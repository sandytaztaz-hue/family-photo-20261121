A candid, photo-realistic kitchen photograph of a family at home with one
machine member — built procedurally in Python (stdlib only) for the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/the-family-photo-with-one-20261121-0246).

## The scene

Late-afternoon window light from the left. A mother stirs a pot at the
stove, back to camera. A father reads a newspaper at the table, the paper
hiding his face. A child on a stool reaches toward the counter. A toddler
plays with wooden blocks on the floor, back to camera. And at the counter,
mid-task, the household robot — matte cream, one warm amber eye — peels a
carrot, a tea towel draped over its forearm.

The one specific observed detail: a half-peeled carrot with peel curls on
the cutting board in the foreground. A dog bowl waits on the floor.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.png` (1024×1024, deterministic — byte-identical
across runs).
